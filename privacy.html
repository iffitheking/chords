Chord Library: privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Chord Library is a guitar songbook app. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores

The songs you add, your setlists and your settings (such as key, capo, scroll speed, notes and favourites) are stored only on your own device, inside the app's storage. They are not uploaded anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your songs or any personal information to the developer or to anyone else.

Microphone

The guitar tuner uses the microphone only while the tuner screen is open, to measure the pitch of the string you play. The sound is analysed on your device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Files you share

Files leave the app only when you choose to export or share them yourself (for example a song backup or a PDF songbook). Where they go is up to you.

Children

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
